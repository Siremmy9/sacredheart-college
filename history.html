Our history

A story rooted in faith, education and a belief in every child.

A beginning with a purpose

Eaglesworld Schools was founded in 2011 by Mrs. Ajisafe Dorcas, a seasoned educationist, with a vision to raise excellent and morally upright people, leaders and world changers.

The school began with one building of six classrooms on a plot of land in Bashorun Estate, Fara Park, Ajah, Lagos. Mrs. Agbale Onome served as Head Teacher, sharing the founding purpose of helping children grow in learning and character.

The purpose that guides us

From the beginning, Eaglesworld was founded on Biblical principles and a commitment to address moral and academic challenges through education. That purpose continues to shape the way we support children, nurture their talents and encourage them to become responsible leaders.

Today, the school provides a learning path through Crèche, Nursery, Primary and Secondary education. Our founding vision remains at the heart of that journey: “Home of Leaders... Where The Future Begins!”
