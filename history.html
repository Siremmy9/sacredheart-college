Our history

A story rooted in faith, education and a belief in every child.

A beginning with a purpose

A 2017 interview published by Tribune Online identifies September 2001 as the establishment date of Sacred Heart Catholic College, Ijebu-Ode. The school was established to provide secondary education within the Catholic educational tradition.

The College operates within the educational network of the Catholic Diocese of Ijebu-Ode. This connects the school to the wider Catholic mission of education, which emphasizes intellectual development, faith, discipline, service, and ethical conduct.

The purpose that guides us

From the beginning, Sacred Heart was founded on Biblical principles and a commitment to address moral and academic challenges through education. That purpose continues to shape the way we support children, nurture their talents and encourage them to become responsible leaders.

Today, the school provides a learning path through Secondary education. Our founding vision remains at the heart of that journey: “Home of Leaders... Where The Future Begins!”
